Campus Lost & Found Management System
1. Introduction
1.1 Project Title
Campus Lost & Found Management System
1.2 Project Overview
The Campus Lost & Found Management System is a web-based platform designed to improve the process of reporting, tracking, matching, verifying, and returning lost items within a college campus.
At present, the college's lost-and-found process is primarily handled through a faculty coordinator. A student who loses an item sends the item's photograph, description, location, date, and other details to the coordinator. The coordinator then forwards the information to an official group. This approach is simple but has several limitations:
* Lost-item messages can get buried among other messages.
* There is no centralized record of all lost and found items.
* Students cannot easily track the status of their report.
* There is no systematic method to connect found items with previously reported lost items.
* It is difficult to measure how many items were found, received by the administration, or successfully returned.
* Students who find an item may not know whether someone has reported it missing.
* Historical data and recovery statistics are not readily available.
The proposed system converts this informal communication process into a structured and trackable workflow while keeping the existing administrative authority intact.
________________


2. Problem Statement
The current campus lost-and-found process depends heavily on manual communication between students and the faculty coordinator.
The primary problem is not the absence of a reporting mechanism. Students already have a way to report lost objects.
The core problem is the absence of a centralized, trackable recovery system.
The proposed system will provide:
Lost Report → Found Report → Admin Receives Item → Match → Ownership Verification → Return
The system will allow the college to maintain a reliable record of every case and monitor the complete lifecycle of an item.
________________


3. Objectives
The main objectives of the system are:
1. Provide a centralized platform for reporting lost items.
2. Provide a simple mechanism for students to report items they have found.
3. Ensure that found physical items are handed over to the designated administrator.
4. Maintain a complete digital record of every lost-and-found case.
5. Automatically identify possible matches between lost and found reports.
6. Allow the administrator to verify ownership before returning an item.
7. Allow students to track the status of their reports.
8. Reduce dependence on manually searching old group messages.
9. Generate useful statistics about campus lost-and-found activity.
10. Improve the overall recovery and return process without bypassing college rules.

5. Target Users / Actors
The system will have three primary actors.
5.1 Student — Lost Item Reporter
A student who has lost an item.
Responsibilities:
* Submit a lost-item report.
* Provide accurate information.
* Upload photographs where available.
* Monitor case status.
* Respond to ownership-verification requests.
* Collect the item from the designated administrator after successful verification.
________________


5.2 Student — Finder
A student who finds an item.
Responsibilities:
* Report the found item through the platform.
* Provide basic information and photograph.
* Hand over the physical item to the designated administrator.
* Provide the case/reference number during handover where applicable.
The finder will not directly return the item to the suspected owner through the platform.
________________


5.3 Administrator
The administrator is the authorized college representative responsible for managing the physical items and approving the digital workflow.
For the initial deployment, this role may be assigned to the existing faculty coordinator, such as Surya Iyer, subject to college approval.
Responsibilities:
* Review reports.
* Approve or reject reports.
* Receive physical found items.
* Confirm that an item has physically reached the administration.
* Review possible matches.
* Verify ownership.
* Contact users when required.
* Mark items as returned.
* Maintain case records.
* Manage users and reports.
* Access analytics.
________________

12. Automatic Matching System
FR-08 Lost-Found Matching
The system shall compare active lost reports with found reports.
The matching engine may use:
* Item category
* Item name
* Text description
* Location
* Date
* Time
* Brand/model
* Color
* Distinguishing characteristics
* Image similarity
The first implementation may use a weighted scoring algorithm.
Example:
Description similarity     30%
Location similarity        25%
Date/time similarity       15%
Category similarity        10%
Brand/model similarity     10%
Image similarity           10%
                            ----
                            100%


The weighting may be modified after testing with real data.
________________


13. Match Confidence
The system shall assign a match confidence score.
Example:
80–100%  → High-confidence possible match
60–79%   → Medium-confidence possible match
40–59%   → Low-confidence match
<40%     → Do not display as important match


These thresholds are configurable and should be validated experimentally rather than treated as guaranteed accuracy.
The system shall never automatically declare ownership solely from the AI/matching score.
The administrator remains responsible for final verification.
________________

15. Ownership Verification
FR-09 Owner Verification
When a possible match is identified, the administrator shall verify the claimant's ownership.
The system should avoid publicly revealing all identifying information.
Instead, the administrator may ask verification questions based on information that was not displayed publicly.
Examples:
For a bag:
What was inside the front pocket?
For a phone:
Describe the case or wallpaper.
For a calculator:
What identifying mark/scratch does it have?
For an ID holder:
Describe the additional card stored inside.
The administrator shall be able to record:
Verification Passed
or
Verification Failed
________________


16. Privacy and Information Disclosure
The system shall follow the principle of minimum necessary disclosure.
A finder shall not automatically receive:
* Owner's phone number
* Owner's email
* Student ID
* Home address
* Other private information
Similarly, a student reporting a lost item shall not automatically receive the finder's personal information.
Communication should remain mediated by the administrator where necessary.
________________


17. Return/Handover Process
FR-10 Return Process
After ownership verification:
1. Administrator identifies the verified owner.
2. Owner is notified.
3. Owner visits the designated location.
4. Administrator confirms identity.
5. Item is physically handed to the owner.
6. Administrator marks the item as Returned.
7. System records date/time of return.
Optional confirmation:
* Student confirms receipt.
* Administrator records the return.
* Both actions are stored in the case history.

32. Business Rules
BR-01
A found report does not mean the physical item has been received.
BR-02
Only an authorized administrator can mark an item as physically received.
BR-03
Only an authorized administrator can approve the final ownership claim.
BR-04
Students should not be required to directly contact the suspected owner.
BR-05
The system should not expose unnecessary personal information.
BR-06
AI-generated matches are suggestions and cannot independently authorize a return.
BR-07
An item can be marked Returned only after administrator confirmation.
BR-08
Every status change shall be recorded.
BR-09
Unclaimed items should remain traceable rather than being silently removed from the system.
BR-10
The existing college rule that only authorized personnel communicate through the official group shall remain unaffected. The application should complement the existing administrative process rather than bypass it.
________________


33. Exception Handling
The system should handle cases such as:
Duplicate Lost Report
If a student tries to report the same item twice, the system should warn them and allow them to update the existing case where appropriate.
Wrong Information
Admin should be able to correct or request correction of report information.
False Found Report
Admin should be able to reject suspicious or invalid reports.
Item Handed to Admin Without Online Report
Admin should be able to create a Found Item record manually.
This is especially important because some students may simply hand an item to Surya/security without using the application.
No Matching Lost Report
The found item remains in the system as:
Received — Awaiting Owner
Multiple Potential Owners
The administrator should manually verify the claimant using additional identifying questions.
Item Remains Unclaimed
The system should automatically flag the case after a configurable period for administrative review.

Risk 1 — Students Do Not Report Found Items
Mitigation:
Make the reporting process extremely simple and communicate clear instructions for physical handover.

Mitigation:
Use administrator-controlled ownership verification.
________________


Risk 3 — AI Generates Incorrect Matches
Mitigation:
AI only recommends matches. Administrator makes the final decision.
________________


Risk 4 — Students Continue Using WhatsApp Only
Mitigation:
Keep the application simple and integrate it with the existing administrative workflow rather than attempting to eliminate the existing group immediately.
________________


Risk 5 — Admin Workload Becomes Too High
Mitigation:
Provide:
* Automatic matching
* Search/filtering
* Case IDs
* Status automation

45. Suggested Project Definition
Project Name
Campus Lost & Found Management System
One-Line Description
A centralized campus platform that tracks lost and found items from initial report to verified return, with administrator-controlled handover and intelligent matching.
Core Problem
The existing WhatsApp-based process provides communication but does not provide structured tracking, matching, verification, or measurable recovery outcomes.
Core Solution
Digitize the complete lost-and-found lifecycle while keeping the authorized faculty administrator as the trusted intermediary.
Core Innovation
Combining structured case management, physical-handover tracking, ownership verification, and intelligent lost-found matching into a single campus-specific system.
